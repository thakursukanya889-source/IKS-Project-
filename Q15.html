About Indian Knowledge Systems

Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) represent the ancient knowledge and traditions of India. They include science, Ayurveda, yoga, architecture, music, agriculture and philosophy.

Importance of IKS

Indian Knowledge Systems help us understand our history, culture and traditons. They also encourage sustainable living and preserve traditional knowledge.
